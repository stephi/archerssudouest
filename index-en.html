LES ARCHERS DU SUD-OUEST DE MONTRÉAL


We are a non-profit, volunteer managed, indoor archery club that was formed in 2002. Our goal is to provide anyone interested in archery a safe and relaxed environment to enjoy the sport. Our membership includes a variety of hobbyists, hunters, and competitors. We have Traditional, Olympic Recurve, and Compound archers. We also pride ourselves on being home to the best Barebow archers in the province.


Our indoor range offers multiple shooting lanes from 5m to 35m. We have all the standard World Archery Indoor targets available for free to our members. We also have 3D, Lancaster Classic, The Vegas Shoot, and other popular targets available.


We have space for beginners and experienced archers to both shoot comfortably. For beginners we provide a quick and free initiation class, advice, and all the equipment you need to start participating in the sport.



The indoor season will be from September to December 2026 due to the renovation of the Gadbois Center.

More information on the new location of the club for the January to June indoor season will be provided soon.
